# Print and share

- Print renders password-protected documents as images, as the print
  system can't open them.
- Shared copies are deleted after a day, not right away, because
  recipients (mail drafts) may read them later.
- "Open with" (after Share in both sheets) hands the document to another
  PDF app: for what this one doesn't do (annotating, signing, OCR), and as
  the only way to another viewer while this app is the default. A separate
  item, not merged into Share: apps handling both would show twice in one
  chooser, alike but doing different things. It's always a chooser (as the
  default this app would open it again), its own viewer excluded; with a
  single other app the system opens it directly, with none a snackbar says
  so. It passes a read-only copy (Share's, named after the file, kept a
  day), the filled-in version included, also from Home: edits made there
  stay in that app. Protected files ask for their password there.
